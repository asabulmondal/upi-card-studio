# UPI PVC Card Studio

Create personalised UPI payment cards for PVC card printing — right in your browser.

**🌐 Use the tool:** https://asabulmondal.github.io/upi-pvc-studio/

### Features
- 🎨 Multiple card designs and portrait/landscape layouts
- 📱 Generate UPI payment QR codes from your UPI ID
- 🖼️ Add an optional profile photo and contact details
- 📥 Export your card as high-resolution PNG or print-sized PDF
- 🔒 No application account or server upload required; card details are processed in your browser

### Important
Always scan-test the exported QR code with a compatible UPI app and verify the recipient details before printing. Generated QR codes do not independently verify that a UPI ID is active or correct.

UPI PVC Card Studio is an independent project and is not affiliated with NPCI, banks, or UPI apps.
