    Free UPI payment card maker

    UPI Card Studio turns your UPI ID into a printable payment card with a scannable QR code. Add your name and photo, choose a layout and colour, then download a PNG or PDF to print on a PVC card or share.

    1. Enter your name and UPI ID. The QR appears as you type.
    2. Pick a layout, colour and name style in the Design tab.
    3. Scan the QR once to check it, then download your card.
    Is it free, and is my data private?

    It is free. Your name, UPI ID and photo are used only in your browser to draw the card. They are not uploaded to a server.

    Will the QR work with my UPI app?

    The QR uses the standard UPI payment link format, which UPI apps read. A generated QR does not confirm that the UPI ID is active, so always scan-test it before printing.

    What size is the card?

    Exports use the ID-1 size of 85.6 × 54 mm, the same as a bank card. Check your printer's layout requirements before ordering.

    Can I add an amount to the QR?

    Yes. Enter an amount and the QR asks for that fixed amount. Leave it blank so the payer can type any amount.
